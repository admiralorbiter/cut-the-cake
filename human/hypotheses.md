## 1. Primary Empirical Hypotheses

### Hypothesis H1: Unfamiliar Performance Governed by $\mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}}$
Under unfamiliar play (Phase 1: `UNFAMILIAR`), lower reveal-gated tactical margin $\mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}}$ predicts lower human survival rate and worse service performance. Specifically, negative-margin encounters ($\mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}} < 0$) should show substantially reduced clearance success under matched player sensorimotor parameters.

### Hypothesis H2: Familiar Performance Governed by $\mathcal{M}_{\text{preaim}}$
With repeated exposure to the same geometric layout (Phase 3: `FAMILIAR`), clearance behavior shifts toward the pre-aim bound $\mathcal{M}_{\text{preaim}}$. Encounters that are infeasible blind ($\mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}} < 0$) but solvable familiar ($\mathcal{M}_{\text{preaim}} \ge 0$) will exhibit significant survival rescue across blocks.

### Hypothesis H3: Knowledge Advantage Predicts Continuous Performance Improvement
The theoretical knowledge gap $\Delta\mathcal{M}_{\text{knowledge}} = \mathcal{M}_{\text{preaim}} - \mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}}$ predicts continuous clearing improvements:
1. **Lateness Reduction:** $\Delta L_{\text{realized}} = L_{\text{realized}}^{\text{Unfamiliar}} - L_{\text{realized}}^{\text{Familiar}}$ correlates positively with $\Delta\mathcal{M}_{\text{knowledge}}$ ($\text{Spearman } \rho > 0$).
2. **Direct Pre-Aim Shrinkage:** Mean reveal-time aim error reduction $\Delta E^{\text{reveal}} = E^{\text{reveal}}_{\text{Unfamiliar}} - E^{\text{reveal}}_{\text{Familiar}}$ correlates positively with $\Delta\mathcal{M}_{\text{knowledge}}$.

### Hypothesis H4: Separation of Perceived Readability and Perceived Fairness
1. Perceived encounter **Fairness** (1–7) correlates primarily with reveal-gated margin $\mathcal{M}_{\text{reveal}}$ (encounters with negative reveal margin feel like unavoidable ambushes).
2. Perceived encounter **Readability** (1–7) correlates primarily with pre-aim margin $\mathcal{M}_{\text{preaim}}$ and target spatial clarity.

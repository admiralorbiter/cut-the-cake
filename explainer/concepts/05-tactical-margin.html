05 · TACTICAL MARGIN & THE QUICK-DRAW RACE

What does Tactical Margin actually mean?

Tactical Margin is not a physical wall or room shape. It is a stopwatch race: the signed amount of time left over between when your bullet hits the enemy and when the enemy fires back at you.

⏱️ THE PHYSICAL ACTION: WHAT HAPPENS WHEN YOU "CLEAR" A CORNER?

The millisecond you step around a corner and an enemy enters your vision, a quick-draw duel starts at 0 ms. Your body and brain execute four physical steps to eliminate the threat:

THE BOTTLENECK PRINCIPLE · MULTI-THREAT CLEARANCE

Why Tactical Margin is a Bottleneck (Not an Average)

In a room with multiple threats, Tactical Margin is not the average slack across enemies. It is strictly determined by the worst-case threat (the critical bottleneck). If even one threat breaches its deadline, the room is lost.

✓ OPTIMAL SCHEDULE (T1 First → T2 Second)
Per-Target Margins:
Threat 1 (Urgent): D₁ = 380ms, C₁ = 340ms M₁ = +40 ms (Bottleneck!)
Threat 2 (Relaxed): D₂ = 850ms, C₂ = 590ms M₂ = +260 ms
Room Margin: M* = min(+40ms, +260ms) = +40 ms

Even though Threat 2 has a large +260 ms buffer, the room's true Tactical Margin is +40 ms because Threat 1 is the weakest link.

✗ TUNNEL VISION (T2 First → T1 Second)
Per-Target Margins:
Threat 2 (Relaxed): D₂ = 850ms, C₂ = 340ms M₂ = +510 ms
Threat 1 (Urgent): D₁ = 380ms, C₁ = 590ms M₁ = −210 ms (BREACH!)
Room Margin: M = min(+510ms, −210ms) = −210 ms 💀

The player wasted time shooting the easy threat first. Threat 1 breached its deadline by 210 ms, making the encounter lethal.

DESIGN IMPLICATION · THE CAPABILITY ENVELOPE

Tactical Margin Bridges Geometry to Human Player Skill

A room does not have a single absolute clearability in a vacuum; it is clearable relative to a player capability model C = (A, ωaim, p). Tactical Margin measures who can survive the layout:

🏆 Pro Esports Athlete (A = 140 ms, ω = 400°/s)

Fast reaction and snappy crosshair flicking give this player M = +85 ms on high-stress competitive angles.

✓ INSIDE CAPABILITY ENVELOPE
🎮 Casual Player (A = 240 ms, ω = 280°/s)

Standard reaction speed and slower turning turn the exact same room into M = −35 ms (impossible without prior knowledge).

In our formal framework:

  • Lateness Ljπ: For threat j under clearing sequence π, lateness is the difference between weapon service completion Cjπ and hostile response deadline Dj. If Lj > 0, the deadline is breached.
  • Optimal Maximum Lateness L*(γ): The worst lateness across all threats under the optimal clearance permutation π*.
  • Tactical Margin M = −L*: By negating maximum lateness, positive values indicate a temporal safety reserve (M ≥ 0), while negative values indicate an unavoidable deadline failure (M < 0).
  • Engine Deployment Reserve (εdeploy): In our native headless ViZDoom validation (ZDoom 35 Hz logic clock), an empirical safety reserve of εdeploy = 3 tics ≈ 85.7 ms guarantees 100% survival against game engine physics and discrete simulation jitter.

01 · CORNER DISTANCE & PERSPECTIVE ADVANTAGE

Why does backing away from a corner help?

In an FPS, whoever is farther from the corner apex sees the opponent's body first. When you hug the wall, the enemy gets the drop on you. When you back away, you get the drop on the enemy.

✗ HUGGING THE CORNER (ENEMY GETS THE DROP)
⚠️ ENEMY GETS THE DROP ON YOU

Because you are closer to the corner than the enemy, the enemy's vision angle catches your right shoulder before your eyes can clear the wall. The enemy fires before you even see them.

✓ BACKED AWAY (YOU GET THE DROP)
✓ YOU GET THE DROP ON THE ENEMY

Because you are farther from the corner than the enemy, your line of sight clips their shoulder before your body enters their field of view. You acquire the target and fire first.

📐 Mathematical Proof: Why the Farther Player Spots the Shoulder First
tan(θ) = w / d

Consider two combatants with body shoulder half-width w = 0.25 m at distances d1 and d2 from the corner apex:

  • The angle from the corner apex to combatant 1's shoulder is: θ1 = arctan(w / d1)
  • The angle from the corner apex to combatant 2's shoulder is: θ2 = arctan(w / d2)

If you stand far back (d1 > d2), your angular spread θ1 is smaller than the enemy's θ2. As your eye rotates around the corner, your line of sight reaches the enemy's shoulder angle θ2 before their line of sight reaches your shoulder angle θ1. Therefore, the farther player mathematically achieves visual detection first.

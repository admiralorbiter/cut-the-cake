06 · MAP KNOWLEDGE & PRE-ALIGNMENT

What does knowing the map buy you?

Map knowledge does not eliminate the enemy or change the physical walls. It allows you to pay the rotational aiming cost while safely behind solid cover, so your crosshairs are already locked on target at the moment of reveal.

👁️ THE TACTICAL DIFFERENCE: BLIND PEEK VS PRE-AIMED PEEK
❌ Blind Peeker (0 ms Advance Warning)
You step around the corner blind. You spot the enemy, then spend 180 ms turning your crosshair in full view. The enemy shoots you before your crosshair can reach their body.
✓ Map-Knowledge Pre-Aimer (≥ 114 ms Advance Warning)
You rotate your crosshairs to the exact target bearing while safely behind the solid wall. When you peek the corner, you are already aiming at their head → instant shot!

THE THREE EPISTEMIC CLASSES · RESEARCH TAXONOMY

How Geometry Dictates the Need for Map Knowledge

Not all encounters require map knowledge. Our research partitions all procedural combat encounters into three fundamental epistemic classes:

1. BLIND-CLEARABLE (ℓ* = 0 ms)
✓ Clearable Blind (M = +180ms)

The enemy is near your natural forward sightline with a generous deadline. You can react and eliminate the threat on first sight with 0 ms advance warning.

2. KNOWLEDGE-RESCUABLE (0 < ℓ* < ∞)
⚡ Rescuable (ℓ* = 114 ms / 4 tics)

A lethal trap if entered blind (−114 ms deficit), but rendered 100% tactically clearable if the player has at least 114 ms (4 tics) of advance pre-aim warning.

3. OVERLOADED (ℓ* = ∞)
💀 Structurally Overloaded (ℓ* = ∞)

Opposing simultaneous deadlines create an inescapable crossfire. Impossible with gunplay alone even under omniscient pre-aim (M(∞) < 0).

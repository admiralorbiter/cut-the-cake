LESSON 1 · SLICE THE PIE

Distance from the corner controls how fast new angle is revealed.

Player language: back up from the corner and slice it instead of swinging wide.

Research language: the same lateral movement exposes a smaller angular slice when stand-off distance is larger.

θ(x; d) = arctan(x / d)
Δθ = arctan((x + Δx)/d) − arctan(x/d) ≈ Δx/d

Same movement. Less new angle. Geometry controls the rate at which new information enters the fight.

LESSON 6 · MAP KNOWLEDGE

What if you know where to aim before the enemy appears?

Player language: pre-aim the angle because you know the map.
